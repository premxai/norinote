No. 1 · The problem

More applications won't get you more callbacks

Auto apply tools send one resume to every opening and hope something sticks. Nori does the opposite: fewer applications, each one built for the role and checked by you.

Mass applying

A ring of keys tried on every door

  • One generic resume goes to every opening
  • Keywords get stuffed in, whether you have the skill or not
  • Applications go out before you ever see them
  • Roles you can't take still get an application

With Nori

One key, cut for one door

  • A one page resume rewritten for each role
  • Only skills your history backs up, with gaps shown instead of hidden
  • Every application waits for your OK
  • Roles that don't fit your criteria get skipped, and Nori tells you why

Every line on your resume
points back to something
you actually did.

No. 2 · The receipts

Don't take our word for it.
Check the receipts.

Every bullet Nori writes carries a link to the fact behind it. You can open that link before you approve, so nothing reaches a recruiter that you can't stand behind.
