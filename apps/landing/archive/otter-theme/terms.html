Terms

The ground rules for using Nori. Last updated October 2026.

The service

Nori finds roles, tailors resumes from facts you provide, and fills applications. It never submits an application without your approval.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of the facts you give Nori and for every application you approve. Follow the terms of the job boards and employers you apply to.

Billing

The hosted plan costs $9 a month and you can cancel anytime. The open source version is free under the MIT license.

No guarantees

Nori cannot promise interviews or offers. Hiring decisions belong to employers.
