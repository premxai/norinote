FAQ

Before you trust a job agent

The questions people ask us most, answered plainly.

I've applied to dozens of jobs and heard nothing back. Why would this be different?

Mass applying sends the same resume everywhere. Nori sends fewer applications, each one rewritten for the role and checked against the skills it asks for. Nobody can honestly promise interviews. What Nori promises is that every application is aimed and fitted.

How does Nori decide which jobs to apply to?

You set your lanes: titles, locations, remote and a minimum match. Nori searches Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters and skips anything below your bar. For every role it passes on, it says why.

Will Nori make things up on my resume?

No. Every line links back to a fact you gave it. If a role asks for a skill you don't have, Nori shows you the gap instead of covering it up.

Is this just another spam bot?

No. You set a daily cap, every application is tailored to its role, and none of them goes out without your OK.

Will ATS software reject the resume?

Every resume goes through 10 checks before it's sent:

  • Single column, with no tables, images or icons
  • Standard section headings, in order
  • Contact details present
  • Dates on every role
  • Bullets of 8 to 30 words
  • Every bullet starts with an action verb
  • At least 70% of the required skills covered
  • Fits on one page
  • No first person pronouns
  • Plain, parser safe characters
Can I see what Nori changed?

Yes. Before you approve, you see every change and the fact it came from.

Does Nori ever apply without asking me?

No. It fills the form and waits. You approve from the dashboard, Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp, email or MCP.

What does Nori read in my email?

By default it checks up to 25 new messages from the last 24 hours, skipping mail you sent. It looks for recruiter replies, interview requests and verification codes. You can export everything Nori knows about you at any time.
